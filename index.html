01 / ABOUT

Understanding learners. Designing for growth.

My work combines classroom teaching experience with a foundation in education and educational technology. I am interested in understanding how learners learn, identifying individual needs, and designing learning experiences that connect clear goals with practical teaching strategies.

Beyond classroom instruction, I have experience in homeroom leadership, English drama, project-based learning, and the integration of technology into teaching.

I am continuing to develop my practice in personalized learning design, curriculum development, and educational programs.
